# Immigration guidance: the United Arab Emirates

Published guidance that the authorities of the United Arab Emirates issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md). Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

**Nothing published for decision-makers was found for the United Arab Emirates.** The federal residency authority's and labour ministry's sites did not answer from this machine; Dubai's residency directorate publishes applicant pages only. `NOTES.txt` records what was checked.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
