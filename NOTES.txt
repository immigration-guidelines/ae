What exists
- MOHRE's ministerial resolutions (e.g. Ministerial Resolution No. 46 of 2022 on work permits, offer
  letters and employment contract forms; Cabinet Resolution No. 203 of 2022 on e-quotas) are published
  as legal acts on the federal portal u.ae / government.ae, e.g.
  https://u.ae/-/media/Images-2022/MoHRE-resolutions-2022/ArMinisterial-Decree-No--46--of-2022-Regarding-Work-Permits-Job-Offers-and-Employment-Contracts-Form.pdf
  These are legislation, not wanted.
- MOHRE Administrative Resolution No. 38 of 2022 "Concerning the Guidelines for Implementing
  Ministerial Resolution No. 46 of 2022 regarding Work Permits, Offer Letters and Employment Contract
  Forms" (issued 21 June 2022 by the Undersecretary for Human Resources Affairs; repeals Administrative
  Resolution No. 22 of 2022). Looked at in the copy held by the Gulf Labour Markets and Migration
  programme (GLMM, a research archive), which is marked "This is not an official translation":
  https://gulfmigration.grc.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Administrative-Resolution-No.38-of-2022-Concerning-the-Guidelines-for-Implementing-Ministerial-Resolution-No.46-of-2022-Regarding-Work-Permits-and-Offer-Letters-and-Employment-Contract-Forms.pdf
  The 26-page annex is a service catalogue per permit type (service description, duration, channels,
  steps, required documents, fees) - the same content as MOHRE's public service directory
  (https://www.mohre.gov.ae/assets/download/20680003/human-resources-services-eng.aspx). It is
  addressed to establishments, not to the officials who decide, and the only accessible copy is an
  unofficial third-party translation. Not kept. GLMM also holds similar administrative resolutions
  (e.g. No. 46 of 2022 implementing Ministerial Resolution No. 251 of 2022).
- MOHRE publishes press-released "guides" (e.g. July 2026 guide linking work-permit issuance to health
  insurance) aimed at employers.

Searches (web search, November 2026) for ICP/GDRFA circulars, manuals or officer instructions returned
only applicant guidance, news reports and commercial visa-agent pages.

Finding
The UAE publishes no guidance addressed to the officials who decide residence or work-permit cases.
What is public is legislation (federal laws, cabinet and ministerial resolutions), administrative
resolutions whose annexes are service catalogues for employers, and applicant service pages.
Internal ICP/GDRFA/MOHRE instructions are not published and no freedom-of-information route exists.
